Notar

A browser-based composer for melodies and chords. Play the keys or click the staff: notes go in at the cursor, and you hear each one as you write it. Then play it back with precise Web Audio timing. Four octaves, C2 to C6, on a grand staff. Uses Nordic pitch names (H = B).
